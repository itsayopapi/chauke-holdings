Strategic Alliance

Plethora Plastic Engineers

Chauke Group Holdings holds a significant stake in Plethora Plastic Engineers — a forward-thinking plastic manufacturing company. This partnership strengthens our footprint in the manufacturing sector.

Plethora specializes in custom plastic injection molding, product design and sustainable manufacturing solutions for automotive, packaging and consumer goods industries.

Through this strategic alliance, we leverage synergies to deliver comprehensive solutions while promoting sustainable manufacturing practices.
